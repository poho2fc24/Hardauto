HOW A PROJECT MOVES

From site visit to handover.

The same four-stage process runs whether it's one residential gate or a multi-entrance commercial site.

STAGE 01

Site Survey

We measure the opening, check power supply, and assess ground conditions on-site — free of charge, no obligation.

STAGE 02

Engineering

We size the motor, gate structure, and control system to the load, span, and how many times a day it will cycle.

STAGE 03

Installation

Certified technicians install and commission the full system — most residential jobs are finished within a day.

STAGE 04

Maintenance

Scheduled servicing keeps motors, tracks, and sensors running — and catches wear before it becomes a breakdown.
